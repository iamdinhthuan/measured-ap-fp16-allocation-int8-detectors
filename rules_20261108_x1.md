# X1. Exact incremental FP16 cost of head blocks (written 2026-11-08, before any X1 result)

Motivation: a head block's MAC share includes its forced (head-output) convolution, so the published
budget constraint c_F + sum_b c_b x_b <= B counts the cost of F twice for selected head blocks
(conservative). Question: does the exact cost c_b^F = MAC(b \ F) / MAC_total change any selection?
Analysis only (solver re-run on published sensitivities); no engine is built, no image is evaluated.

- Inputs (read only): metrics/alloc_<m>_int8ent_ho.json (published r, cost, force_cost, allocations) for
  m in yolo11s, yolo26s, yolo11l, yolo26l; configs/fx_<m>_int8ent_ho_fsweep.json (F-conditioned r) for
  yolo26l and yolo11l; block regexes of metrics/sens_<m>_int8ent.json.
- Per-convolution MACs: allocate.conv_macs on an FP32 ONNX regenerated from exports/<m>.pt with the
  export settings of m8l_gate.py (opset 17, simplify, batch 1, static, imgsz 640) into exports/x1_tmp/
  (deleted at the end). The archived ONNX files are not needed; MACs depend only on shapes.
- Gate G1 (per model): block costs and force_cost recomputed from the regenerated ONNX equal the
  published values to 1e-9 (absolute MAC share). Failure: the model is reported as not analysable.
- Gate G2 (per model): allocate.solve with the published costs reproduces every published mean@B and
  minimax@B selection (and every fsweep mean@B selection). Failure: reported, no X1 claim for that model.
- X1 solve: same r, same budgets (3, 5, 10, 15, 20%), same solve(), cost vector c_b^F (convolutions of b
  not matched by the forced regex), budget B - c_F. Objectives: mean (primary), minimax (descriptive).
- Report: per model and budget, whether the selection changes, the exact MAC share of published and X1
  selections, and the surrogate-predicted tuning-set AP change (descriptive, not a result on val2017).
- Consequences:
  (a) no mean selection changes on any of the 20 COCO pairs (and none of the 10 fsweep pairs):
      Supplementary S6 and the Scope paragraph state that the exact cost leaves every selection, hence every
      reported AP, unchanged.
  (b) otherwise: the changed pairs are listed with their predicted tuning-set gain, stated as not evaluated
      on val2017 (engines not rebuilt), and the Scope paragraph keeps the conservative-budget wording plus
      the count of changed pairs. No AP claim either way.
- Output (never overwritten): metrics/x1_exactcost.json; log logs/x1_exactcost.log; sha256 in logs/fx.sha256.
