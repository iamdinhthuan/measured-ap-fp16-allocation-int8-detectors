# X2. Held-out AP of the selections changed by exact incremental costs (written 2026-11-08, before any X2 result)

Motivation: X1 (`rules_20261108_x1.md`, `metrics/x1_exactcost.json`) re-solved the allocation with the exact cost
c_b^F = MAC(b \ F)/MAC_total and found that 8 of the 20 published mean selections change (yolo11s 15%, 20%;
yolo26s 5%, 10%, 15%; yolo26l 5%, 10%, 20%) and 5 of the 10 F-restored mean selections (yolo26l 3%, 5%, 10%, 20%;
yolo11l 15%). Their tuning-set gains were only predicted. X2 measures them on COCO val2017 (held out) against the
published selection rebuilt in the same session. Minimax selections are not evaluated (the article reports them
descriptively).

## 0. Reconstruction (no published file is overwritten; all new outputs use new tags)
- val2017 images and annotations: read-only symlinks to topic_c (as in M8L). Calibration: the 512 published
  calibration images (`m8l_calib_files.txt`) downloaded again from images.cocodataset.org. No tuning images.
- FP32 ONNX re-exported from exports/<m>.pt with the M8L settings (opset 17, simplify, batch 1, static, 640);
  INT8 Q/DQ ONNX re-quantized with the published settings (ModelOpt, entropy, 512 images, Sigmoid excluded).
- Gate G0 (per model): the block partition of the regenerated INT8 graph equals the published one
  (metrics/sens_<m>_int8ent.json). A model failing G0 (or whose regeneration crashes) is reported as not
  reproduced and not evaluated. For yolo11l the INT8 sha256 is compared, descriptively, with 630e5cb8... (the
  M8L regeneration in logs/fx.sha256, not the originally published graph, whose digest was never recorded).
- Check G3 (per pair): the rebuilt published selection is compared with its published val2017 AP; a deviation
  above 0.07 AP (the documented YOLO build spread) is flagged and reported next to the pair, which stays in its
  Holm family (both engines of a pair are rebuilt in the same session).

## 1. Engines and evaluation
- Exact-cost plans: configs/x2_<m>_exact.json (copy of the published plan, resp. the F-restored plan, with the
  changed mean@B allocations replaced by the X1 "exact" blocks). Tags: x2e_ (published arm), x2fe_ (F-restored arm).
  Rebuilt published selections: x2r_ (published arm), x2fr_ (F-restored arm). Built and evaluated by eval_alloc.py
  (same builder, same val2017 inference). FP32 val predictions: metrics/x2/fp32_<m>_val.json (yolo11l reuses
  metrics/m8l/fp32_val.pred.json.gz).
- Paired image-level bootstrap (bootstrap_fast.py, n=1000, default seed) of exact minus rebuilt published,
  per changed pair. Output metrics/bootstrap/x2_<m>.json.

## 2. Decision rule (primary family: the 8 published-arm pairs; Holm over 8 at alpha=0.05,
   two-sided p = max(2 min(p_hat, 1-p_hat), 2/1001), reject when p <= alpha/(m-k))
- (a) No pair significant after Holm: the article states that exact costs change 8 of 20 mean selections but
  change held-out AP by [min, max] with no significant difference, and keeps the conservative budget.
- (b) At least one exact-cost selection significantly BETTER: the article reports the pair(s) and gains, states that
  the conservative budget cost up to that AP, and keeps the published results (no re-run of other tables).
- (c) At least one exact-cost selection significantly WORSE and none better: reported as such (the larger budget
  did not help).
- The restored MAC shares of both selections (exact incremental MACs from metrics/x1_exactcost.json, same basis
  for both) are reported next to every difference. Engine latencies recorded by eval_alloc.py are not reported.
- The comparison isolates the cost model on the measured method; Table 2 compares all methods under the same
  conservative cost model, and no exact-cost selection is compared with a conservative-cost baseline.
- Secondary family: the 5 F-restored pairs, Holm over 5, reported in Supplementary Table S12 only; no claim in the
  main text beyond the range.
- Whatever the outcome, no other result of the article is changed and no selection is re-tuned on val2017.

## 3. Housekeeping
- At most two heavy jobs (quantization queue on CPU, evaluation queue on GPU). New tags only; sha256 of every output
  appended to logs/fx.sha256. Reconstructed data/ and regenerated ONNX deleted after the run (metrics, logs and
  predictions kept).
