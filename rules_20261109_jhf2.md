# JHF2. Same-session Jetson latency of head-first SQNR and measured allocations of YOLO26-l on TensorRT 10.16
(written 2026-11-09 13:21 +07, before any JHF2 engine was built; replaces JHF)

## History
- JHF (rules_20261109_jhf.md) started 10:27 on the Jetson with the Table 5 software (L4T R36.5, TensorRT 10.3). Between
  ~11:30 and 12:12 the Jetson was reflashed by its owner to L4T R39.2.1 (JetPack 7) with TensorRT 10.16; the JHF directory,
  its partial results and the Table 5 engine files on the device were erased, and no JHF result was ever read. JHF is
  abandoned. Table 5 (TensorRT 10.3) cannot be re-measured on this device, and its engines cannot be loaded by TensorRT 10.16.

## 0. Graphs (jhf2_export.py)
- All graphs derive from the hp32 Jetson base of Table 5 (base.onnx, sha256 65c7e25f...). Gate (passed before this rule was
  written): headout (INT8 + F), head (whole head FP16), mean_B0.05, mean_B0.1 and mean_B0.2 re-derived with the manifest
  regexes equal the Table 5 graphs byte for byte (sha256 in metrics/orin_latency/yolo26l_manifest.json). Head-first graphs
  hf_B0.1, hf_B0.15, hf_B0.2: regexes of metrics/alloc_eval/yolo26l_int8ent/fxh_hyb_B{b}.json (182, 277, 325 restored DQ
  inputs, as on the desktop). rb_mean_B0.1 and rb_mean_B0.2 are copies of mean_B0.1/mean_B0.2, built separately to give
  the build-to-build latency spread. No FP16 reference (its FP32 export no longer exists).

## 1. Session
- Power mode MAXN_SUPER, set by the owner (the assistant never runs nvpmodel or jetson_clocks), checked read-only before the
  first build and after the last timing (/var/lib/nvpmodel/status = pmode:0002, cpu0 scaling_max_freq = 1728000). If a check
  fails, the session is reported with the observed mode and the deviation stated.
- All 11 engines built in one session with trtexec --int8 --fp16 (TensorRT 10.16) and timed with jhf_bench_gated.sh
  (= orin_bench_gated.sh: idle-GPU and foreign-trtexec gating, 3 interleaved rounds, --warmUp=1000 --duration=20
  --useSpinWait, median GPU compute time, VDD_IN; power mode read from /var/lib/nvpmodel/status). Directory
  ~/isci_orin/yolo26l_jhf2. A build or timing failure is reported as such.

## 2. Reporting
- Per engine: median of the three round medians, change vs. the same-session INT8 engine, mean VDD_IN, energy per frame,
  engine size. AP is the desktop COCO val2017 AP of the same selection (Table 5 / Supplementary Table S8).
- Table 5 is not changed. The session is reported in the Supplementary as a separate session on different software, with all
  same-session numbers. Main text: the limitation "head-first allocations were not timed" becomes a statement that they were
  timed only in this separate session, with one clause giving the head-first vs. measured latency difference at 10 and 20%.
- Comparison head-first vs. measured at B = 10 and 20% (same session): difference in ms and % of the measured engine.
  Descriptive, no test. A difference not larger than the build-to-build spread (max |rb_mean - mean| / mean over the two
  budgets) is described as within the build-to-build spread. As a consistency note only, the same-session changes vs. INT8 of
  the five Table 5 graphs are set beside the Table 5 changes.
